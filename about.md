  <h1>About</h1>

  <p>I'm Francis Phan. This site is two portfolios sharing one spine.</p>

  <h2>Photographs</h2>

  <p>Travel and street photography — the Bolivian altiplano and its salt flats, night markets and neon in Taiwan and Korea, tigers and street color across India. The <a href="/#portfolio">galleries</a> are organized the way the trips felt: a selected portfolio, the cities, and India on its own.</p>

  <h2>Code</h2>

  <p>By day I build software for a wine hotel in Mendoza, Argentina — the integrations between its property-management system, Salesforce, and NetSuite, and the AI agents that sit on top of them. On my own time I build tools I want to exist. Both halves live on the <a href="/projects/">projects page</a> and on <a href="https://github.com/francisphan/">GitHub</a>.</p>
